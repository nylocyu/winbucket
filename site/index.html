Reviews are annual. Memory isn't.

The compliment a colleague left in Slack in February is gone by March — buried, or the channel's archived. The project you shipped in Q1 blurs into the one from Q3. By review time you remember the last six weeks and call it your year.

How it works

  1. 1. Drop it in

    Drag a screenshot, file or email onto the menu bar icon. Two seconds, no form.

  2. 2. Add a line

    One note on what it was and why it mattered. The on-device AI can turn a bullet point into a full sentence.

  3. 3. Export when it counts

    One click gives you a Markdown file and every attachment, ready to bring to the review.

Features

Menu bar drop target

Drop a file straight onto the icon and it's saved instantly. Hover while dragging to open the popover instead.

Note, link and attachment

A free-text note, an optional link, and any file, screenshot or folder — copied into your bucket with its original filename.

On-device AI rewrite

A sparkle button turns terse bullet points into full sentences, using Apple's on-device model. It only rephrases what you wrote — no invented facts, numbers or names.

Markdown export

One button produces a ZIP: a chronological WinBucket.md plus every attachment in an Anhänge folder.

Your folder, your files

Wins live in a folder you choose, as a plain wins.json and an Attachments folder. Move it into iCloud Drive or Dropbox for your own sync.

30-day trash

Delete a win by mistake and recover it within 30 days. After that it's purged automatically.

Privacy

  • No account, no server, no sync — the app contains no networking code.

  • Your wins live in a folder you choose, as wins.json plus an Attachments folder. Plain files you can read, back up, or delete.

  • The AI rewrite runs on your Mac via Apple's on-device model. Your notes never leave the machine.

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry.

  • Open source — read the code yourself.

Requirements & install

  • macOS 26 or later. Apple silicon.
  • The AI rewrite requires Apple Intelligence to be available. Everything else works without it.

Win Bucket is signed but not notarized yet. On first launch macOS will warn you: right-click the app → Open → Open again. You only do this once.

Prefer to build it yourself:

git clone https://github.com/nylocyu/winbucket
cd winbucket
./Scripts/build_app.sh && open WinBucket.app

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, free and open source.

Where is my data stored?

In a folder you pick on first launch. Change it anytime by right-clicking the menu bar icon.

Can I sync between Macs?

Not built in — but point your bucket folder at iCloud Drive or Dropbox and it syncs itself.

Does the AI make things up?

No. It's instructed to rephrase only what you wrote — no added facts, numbers or names — and it runs entirely on your Mac.

Windows or iPhone version?

No. It's a native Mac app.
